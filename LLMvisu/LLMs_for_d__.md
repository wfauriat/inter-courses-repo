LLMs are complex machines, built though training on huge corpora of text and, to do one thing : predict the next word, right after any piece of text that is fed into them. Through training, they pick up statistical regularities, and become good at predicting which next word would be more likely.

Decoder-style (such as GPT) LLMs are complex generative machines, built though training on huge corpora of text and, to do one thing : predict the next word, right after any piece of text that is fed into them. Through training, they pick up statistical regularities, and become good at predicting which next word would be more likely. They sample a next word out of a list of plausibles, the one-word longer sequence is fed back so that the process may be repeated, progessively generating text of any desired length.

--> 

Decoder-style (such as GPT) LLMs are complex generative machines, built though training on huge corpora of text and, to do one thing : predict the next token, right after any piece of text that is fed into them. Through training, they pick up statistical regularities, and become good at predicting which next token would be more likely. They sample a next token out of a list of plausibles, the one-token longer sequence is fed back so that the process may be repeated, progessively generating text of any desired length.

-->

Decoder-style (such as GPT) LLMs, are essentially, sequential next-token producers.
