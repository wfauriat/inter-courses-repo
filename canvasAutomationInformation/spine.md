# Structural spine of the corpus

Nine nodes carrying the argument. Drop any one and the rest can't recover what's lost. They form a complete legitimate path from any entry — what every orientation-specific path has to make visible, even when it's not what the path is *about*.

## 1. Why care (the stake)

**`world_responds_to_action`** — The world responds to the action taken, not to the picture that produced it. Anticipations matter to the agent; the world does not consult them. *(Without this, the project reads as philosophy of mind rather than reasoning under stakes.)*

## 2. The structural setup

**`frame_problem`** — Relevance has to be decided upstream of any inference. To decide what does matter, one needs an idea of what could matter; the choice is upstream of the analysis it would inform.

**`finite_specification`** — Every prediction works on a finite specification of conditions. What it omits does not appear as wider intervals; it does not appear at all. *(The hub — every cluster routes through here.)*

**`three_kinds_of_uncertainty`** — Aleatory (irreducible-by-choice), epistemic (reducible-with-information), **ontological** (not in the model because no one thought to put it there). The third category is what the structural problem makes visible and what formal practice routinely underweights.

**`frame_applies_to_both_modes`** — The problem applies to deduction and induction alike. Deduction's necessity is necessity-inside-the-frame; induction's pattern-fit is pattern-fit-inside-the-frame. Exact prediction is precluded structurally, not contingently.

**`frame_invisible_from_inside`** — A reasoning system reasons from within a frame; it is structurally blind to its own blind spots. Auditing the frame requires resources the frame does not carry. *(The punch — without this, the problem reads as "harder than it looks" rather than structurally unsolvable.)*

## 3. The escape foreclosed

**`meta_no_free_lunch`** — No formal procedure decides when a frame is adequate, because any such procedure is itself a frame and inherits the problem one level up. "Better methodology" does not close the gap; the methodology is the choice being deferred. *(Without this, every structural claim is provisional pending a better procedure.)*

## 4. Why now (the intensification)

**`automation_opacity`** — The structural problem is not new; the conditions are. Increased automation of information processing decreases reflexivity on chosen treatments; the practitioner becomes faster while the system becomes harder to inspect. The disposition the corpus argues for does not change; the intensity with which it must be held does.

## 5. The destination

**`humility_as_disposition`** — Epistemic humility is what the corpus argues for. Structural incentives reward confident expertise; psychology resists provisional belief on identity and on display; scientific training is itself a poor inducement. Naming the pressure is part of holding the disposition. *(Without this, the structural floor has no normative landing.)*

---

## The arc, in one sentence

Decisions have stakes (1), reasoning runs on a frame whose relevance and specification are necessarily finite (2–4), the limit applies even to formal deduction (5) and the system cannot audit it from within (6), no meta-procedure dissolves the regress (7), automation now raises the cost of failing to hold the disposition (8), so humility is not a stylistic preference but a structural commitment (9).
