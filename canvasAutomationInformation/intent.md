# Argument Canvas — Intent

## What it is

A tool for building and delivering arguments on an infinite spatial canvas, where the argument materializes card by card as the path is walked. At session start, nothing is shown but an entry point. The visible canvas is the trace of a specific walk, not a map of the whole.

The same engine carries two delivery modes — a *speaker mode* in which a presenter walks a prepared path with room to step aside and return, and a *self-paced mode* in which an explorer clicks their way through. The mode changes who is driving; it does not change what the engine does.

The artifact does not insist on a single thesis, a single arc, or a single opening. The same library admits several plausible paths for different audiences, several legitimate destinations, and several possible hooks into the material.

## Commitments

- *Latent graph, emergent layout.* Cards and the relations between them exist in the library; the canvas only shows what has been walked. Past cards persist; the spatial trace *is* the record of the walk.
- *Spatial semantics carry argumentative meaning.* The position of a card relative to its parent says something about how it relates — supporting, opposing, qualifying, reconciling. The exact orientations are not pre-locked; they are choices left open for the design to instantiate.
- *Path as artifact.* A walked path — a talk delivered, an exploration completed, a saved tour — is itself an object that can be recorded, replayed, compared.
- *Argument made by walking it.* The audience that reaches a conclusion through their own clicks experiences the argument differently than one that is led through it. This difference is the central design bet on lowering defended resistance.

## Tensions

- *Emergence vs orientation.* Hiding the map is what gives the walk its drive; it is also what makes losing one's place possible. Wayfinding has to do work the map would otherwise do.
- *Audience-driven vs author-led.* More walker freedom means less rhetorical control. Multiple legitimate destinations is the resolution, not a workaround.
- *Coherence vs plurality.* Many audiences, destinations, openings — but it must still feel like one artifact. The unity comes from the shared library and the meaning of the spatial relations, not from a forced single thesis.
- *Resistance vs guidance.* The walk is suggested rather than free — proposed connections lead toward load-bearing passage points the audience is expected to visit (the central claims and the moves that earn them), with side excursions permitted around that backbone rather than instead of it. The bet is that a suggested path the audience chooses to follow still bypasses more defenses than one they are led through; the cost is that suggestions must be strong enough to be taken without collapsing back into the linear form.
